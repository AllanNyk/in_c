In C — controls

A browser realization of Terry Riley's In C (1964). You conduct an autonomous ensemble — spawn voices, shape their dynamics, push them through the 53 figures.

Spawning & ensemble

  • click empty canvas — first click starts the ostinato + spawns voice 1; later clicks spawn the next voice from the roster (after the cooldown ring fills around the ostinato)

Per voice (hover a colored circle)

  • click advance one figure · ctrl+click advance 5
  • right-click reverse one figure · ctrl+right-click reverse 5
  • shift+click — align every other voice to this voice's current figure
  • M mute · R lock current figure (auto-advance off)
  • scroll per-voice volume
  • ← → swap to previous / next instrument in the roster

Ostinato (hover the central circle)

  • click shift pitch up · right-click shift pitch down (C4–C6)
  • M mute · scroll volume · H hide visually (audio keeps playing)

Global

  • Top bar: master volume · tempo (60–200 BPM) · Random · Conclude · About · ?
  • R when not hovering — reroll all unlocked voices to random figures (unlocked once all 11 voices are spawned)
  • Conclude appears once every voice has reached the final figure — pressing it begins the dismantling end
  • In the dismantling phase, click any voice or the ostinato to remove it for good. When everything is gone the piece concludes.
  • ? toggle this help panel

Visual feedback

  • Background fades white→dark as the ensemble spreads across many figures
  • Gold strands link voices that share a figure (unison)
  • Colored sparkles flash where notes from different voices coincide in time

Press Esc or click outside to close.
